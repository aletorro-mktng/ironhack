## Requirements

Write in the **third person**. Lead with credibility (awards, publishing achievements, relevant expertise). Add a touch of personality and end with a clear call to action (website, newsletter, social, upcoming release). Avoid résumé-style length, excessive self-promotion, and too many links.

Produce three tailored versions, each labeled:

- **Short bio (15–30 words)** — for social media and guest-post bylines.
- **Standard bio (50–100 words)** — for Amazon, press releases, and book jackets.
- **Long bio (200–250 words)** — for websites, media kits, and speaking engagements.

Use only verifiable facts from the knowledge base.

## Output Instructions

Return all three labeled bios.

## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective: brand authority, organic growth, performance enablement, or community value.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage: discover, consider, or decide.
- The clearest CTA.
- The emotional hook.
- The save/share value.

Use one clear idea. Do not create daily noise. Make the post feel native to Instagram.

## Instagram Setup

The content request may include an **Instagram post format**, **Instagram hashtag strategy**, and **Instagram opening hook**.

- Tailor the caption to the post format: a Reel/Story caption is shorter and punchier; a carousel caption can reference "swipe"; a single image post is self-contained.
- Follow the hashtag strategy exactly: if "no hashtags", include none; otherwise match the requested count and mix. Place hashtags after the caption body.
- If an opening hook is supplied, open with it (lightly polished if needed). If none is supplied, write a strong cinematic hook yourself.

## Audience Interpretation

The content request separates audience intent into reader/platform audiences and role/editorial audiences.

- Reader/platform audiences such as BookTok readers, Bookstagram readers, influencers, potential readers, new readers, existing fans, YA thriller readers, horror readers, dark academia readers, and general audience are discovery audiences.
- If hashtags are requested, choose hashtags those discovery audiences actually use, browse, or recognize. Prioritize audience-native tags such as book community, genre, trope, reader identity, and platform culture tags over generic promotional tags.
- Role/editorial audiences such as journalists, reviewers, and ARC readers are message-framing audiences.
- When those role audiences are selected, write the caption to address their likely questions, concerns, and interests: news value, credibility, verified proof, spoiler safety, review usefulness, audience fit, access, and clear next steps.

## Caption Requirements

- Open with a strong cinematic hook.
- Sound specific to the Mortal Vengeance universe.
- Use the brand voice from the knowledge base.
- Avoid generic thriller marketing language.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Create tension without spoiling major plot twists.
- End with a question or clean call to action.
- Match the requested hashtag strategy (default to 3–6 relevant hashtags if none is specified).

## Quality Check

Before returning the caption, verify:

- The objective is clear.
- The hook works immediately.
- The caption does not sound generic.
- The CTA matches the audience intent.
- Hashtags, when present, fit the selected reader/platform audiences.
- Copy for journalists, reviewers, or ARC readers addresses their practical concerns instead of only selling the book.
- Any factual claim comes from the knowledge base.
- Any real review quote comes only from the real reviews file.

## Output Instructions

Return only the final caption.

## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective: brand authority, organic growth, performance enablement, or community value.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage: discover, consider, or decide.
- The clearest CTA.
- The emotional hook.
- The save/share value.

Use one clear idea. Do not create daily noise. Make the post feel native to Instagram.

## Caption Requirements

- Open with a strong cinematic hook.
- Sound specific to the Mortal Vengeance universe.
- Use the brand voice from the knowledge base.
- Avoid generic thriller marketing language.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Create tension without spoiling major plot twists.
- End with a question or clean call to action.
- Include 3 to 6 relevant hashtags.

## Quality Check

Before returning the caption, verify:

- The objective is clear.
- The hook works immediately.
- The caption does not sound generic.
- The CTA matches the audience intent.
- Any factual claim comes from the knowledge base.
- Any real review quote comes only from the real reviews file.

## Output Instructions

Return only the final caption.

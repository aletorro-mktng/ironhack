## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective: brand authority, organic growth, performance enablement, or community value.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage: discover, consider, or decide.
- The best angle for the selected channel.
- The clearest CTA.
- Whether the content is useful, specific, and aligned with the brand.

Use the editorial flow when relevant:

1. Problem / Tension
2. Context / Education
3. Solution
4. CTA

Do not produce content just because the format was requested. The output must have a clear strategic purpose.

Apply the playbook’s quality standards:

- Human review is expected before publishing.
- Brand voice must be manually enforceable.
- Factual or canon claims must come from the knowledge base.
- Real review quotes must only come from the real reviews file.
- The final content should feel native to the selected platform.

## Pull Quote Rules

Separate outputs into two categories when relevant:

1. Real Review Excerpts:
Use only quotes that appear in the knowledge base under real reviews or editorial quotes. Do not invent reviewer names, publications, ratings, awards, or endorsements.

2. Brand-Created Promo Pull Quotes:
You may generate original marketing pull quotes, but label them as brand-created promo lines, not real reviews.

## Requirements

- Keep each quote concise and punchy.

Before returning the quotes, verify:

- Real quotes are copied only from the real reviews file.

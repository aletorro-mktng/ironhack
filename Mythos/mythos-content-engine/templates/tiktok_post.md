## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage.
- The fastest hook.
- The single idea the post should communicate.
- The reason someone would watch, save, share, or comment.

TikTok content should be vertical-first, fast, clear, and built for the first 2 to 3 seconds.

## TikTok Requirements

- Open with an immediate hook.
- Make the premise clear in the first line.
- Use fast, sharp, emotionally charged language.
- Sound specific to Mortal Vengeance.
- Avoid generic thriller phrases.
- Include a short caption.
- Include an optional on-screen text idea.
- Include 5 to 8 relevant hashtags.

## Quality Check

Before returning the post, verify:

- The hook is immediate.
- The concept works without needing too much explanation.
- The post has one clear idea.
- The language feels native to TikTok.
- The content does not reveal spoilers unless requested.
- Any real review quote comes only from the real reviews file.

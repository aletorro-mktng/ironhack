## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage.
- The emotional reason this character matters.
- The CTA or engagement question.

The spotlight should provide community value and brand authority by helping readers understand the character without flattening them into a trope.

## Character Spotlight Requirements

Focus on:

- What the character wants.
- What the character hides.
- What the character fears.
- What the character might become.
- Their emotional contradiction.
- Their role in the Mortal Vengeance world.

The tone should be cinematic, sharp, and emotionally specific.

Avoid spoilers unless the user explicitly asks for them.

## Quality Check

Before returning the spotlight, verify:

- The character feels specific.
- The spotlight has emotional tension.
- The content does not become a plain summary.
- The CTA invites engagement or curiosity.
- Any canon claim comes from the knowledge base.

Return only the final character spotlight.

## Requirements

Produce a clear, designer-ready brief with these labeled sections:

- **Concept** — the single core idea the image must communicate.
- **Mood & tone** — atmosphere, genre cues (dark literary suspense, Caribbean Gothic, cinematic contrast).

- **Color & typography** — palette direction (burgundy, ivory) and title treatment notes.
- **Format & specs** — aspect ratios/sizes needed (e.g. ebook cover, social crops).
- **Do / Don't** — must-haves and things to avoid (no generic stock look, no watermarks, no platform logos).

## Requirements

A book description is a sales page disguised as a story teaser. Aim for **150–250 words**.

Use the three-part formula:

- **Hook** — open with a compelling question, mystery, or bold statement.
- **Conflict** — introduce the protagonist and the central problem, establish the stakes. Never reveal major twists or the ending.
- **Tease** — end with a question, impossible choice, or looming threat.

Formatting: short paragraphs, strategic bolding, mobile-friendly. Naturally weave in genre/audience SEO phrases (e.g. Young Adult Horror, Psychological Thriller, Caribbean Gothic, Dark Academia) only where they fit.

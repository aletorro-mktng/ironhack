## Requirements

The goal is to start a conversation, not close a deal. Keep the email under 200 words.

Produce:

- **Subject line** — short, direct, specific (no clickbait, no all-caps, no generic sales language).
- **Opening** — show genuine familiarity with the recipient (reference a recent article, project, or shared interest).
- **Value proposition** — why you are reaching out, why it matters to them, why their audience cares. Short paragraphs only.
- **Call to action** — one simple next step (e.g. offer a review copy or a ten-minute chat).
- **Follow-up note** — a one-line reminder to follow up in three to four business days.

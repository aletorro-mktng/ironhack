## Content Strategy Rules

Before writing, use the content playbook to determine:

- The main objective.
- The audience and intent.
- The decision stage.
- The one message of this email.
- The value exchange for the reader.
- The CTA.

Email is not a dumping ground. Every send must earn its place in the inbox.

## Newsletter Requirements

- Sound direct, warm, and slightly conspiratorial.
- Make the reader feel close to the story.
- Keep the blurb concise.
- Include one strong emotional hook.
- Mention Mortal Vengeance context when relevant.
- Avoid generic promotional hype.
- End with a clear reason to click, read, reply, or engage.

## Quality Check

Before returning the blurb, verify:

- There is one clear message.
- The reader gets something valuable.
- The CTA is specific.
- The tone feels personal but still brand-aligned.
- Any factual claim comes from the knowledge base.
- Any real review quote comes only from the real reviews file.

## Output Instructions

Return only the final newsletter blurb.

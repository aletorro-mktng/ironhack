## Requirements

A press kit should function as a complete resource center for media, partners, reviewers, and event organizers. Produce a checklist (use checkbox bullets like `- [ ]`) grouped under these headings:

- **Company / Author Overview** — elevator pitch, mission, origin story.
- **Leadership / Author Bios** — professional bios, high-resolution headshots.
- **Facts & Statistics** — founding/launch date, audience size, awards, growth metrics.
- **Branding Assets** — logos, brand guidelines, fonts, color codes.
- **Media Assets** — book covers, product photos, promotional and editorial-use images.
- **Press Coverage** — press releases, media mentions, reviews, interviews.
- **Contact Information** — media contact, email, phone, website.

Add a short **Best Practices** note (host on the website, easy downloads, review quarterly).

## Output Instructions

Return the finished press kit checklist.

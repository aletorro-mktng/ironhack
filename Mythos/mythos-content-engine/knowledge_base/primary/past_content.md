## New Thriller Mortal Vengeance Explores the Deadly Cost of Silence

### Dominican Author Alejandro Torres De la Rocha Explores Guilt, Power, and the Machinery of Silence in His Breakout Thriller Series

**Barcelona, Spain - November 17, 2025** - When elite students at Santo Domingo's most prestigious Catholic school set out to expose their sadistic teacher, they didn't just uncover a monster-they created one. *Mortal Vengeance*, the haunting debut by Alejandro Torres De la Rocha, is a sharp, psychologically charged thriller about complicity, cowardice, and the institutional silence that allows abuse to thrive.

Originally released on Amazon/Kindle in June 2025 (ebook, paperback, and hardcover), the novel now reaches a wider global audience. Starting November 17, the ebook edition is available worldwide on Google Play Store, Barnes & Noble, and Kobo.

**To mark the expansion, the ebook will be free to download from November 17 through November 30 on all platforms except Amazon.** At Barnes & Noble, readers may use promo code **BNPMV4FREE** to redeem their copy.

*Mortal Vengeance* has already earned recognition as a Best Seller in the Thriller genre by BookRaid and is currently on the **SHORT LIST of the 2025 Dante Rossetti Book Awards for Young Adult Fiction**.

Early praise and award recognition have positioned *Mortal Vengeance* as one of the year's standout YA thrillers.

## A Pressure Cooker of Guilt and Consequences

Set in the suffocating halls of Excelsior Academy, an elite Catholic school in Santo Domingo, *Mortal Vengeance* follows a group of friends whose carefully planned revenge spirals into chaos. After a brutal prank goes wrong, their school becomes a pressure cooker of guilt, fear, and buried truths. As tensions rise and consequences mount, it becomes clear: silence has a price, and someone is making sure they pay it.

"I wanted to explore the complicity of silence," says Torres De la Rocha, who attended a Jesuit school for twelve years. "Turning away doesn't make you innocent-it makes you part of the machine that protects abusers."

While deeply rooted in Dominican culture-its power structures, its social hierarchies, and its folklore-the novel's themes are universal: abuse of authority, guilt, moral cowardice, and the systems that look the other way. In the tradition of *Elite*, *A Good Girl's Guide to Murder*, and *I Know What You Did Last Summer*, *Mortal Vengeance* blends tense emotional stakes with sharp social commentary.

## About the Author

Alejandro Torres De la Rocha is a Dominican-born, Barcelona-based writer whose twelve years in a Jesuit school taught him everything he needed to know about institutional complicity. *Mortal Vengeance* is his first published novel.

## A Growing Universe

Mortal Vengeance marks the beginning of a planned slasher trilogy, each book tracing the shifting dynamics, dangers, and survivors that emerge after every reckoning. Running parallel to the trilogy is the Grim Saga, a series of canon prequels that expand and deepen the mythology of the Mortal Vengeance Universe. The first entry, *Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale (Book 0)*, is set 18 months before Book 1 and examines both the cultural and psychological roots of the Grim Cojuelo, as well as the institutional rot at Excelsior Academy that feeds into the events of the main story. New chapters release weekly on Wattpad, with the final chapter arriving on January 1, 2026.

The next installment in the trilogy-*Mortal Vengeance II*-is currently underway, and a sequel to *Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale* is also planned.

# From Slasher to Psychological "Howdunit": The Mortal Vengeance Prequel Arrives

## A dark academia, Caribbean Gothic origin story exploring institutional silence, folklore, and the cost of looking away.

**BARCELONA, SPAIN** - *Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale*, the highly anticipated prequel to Alejandro Torres De la Rocha's award-recognized thriller *Mortal Vengeance*, is now available worldwide in ebook and print through Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and directly via Books by AleTorro.

The original novel received the Gold Book Award from Literary Titan and was long-listed for the Dante Rossetti Book Awards for Young Adult Fiction. Early critical reception for *A Grim Tale* continues that momentum, earning 5 Gold Stars from Reader Views.

While *Mortal Vengeance* unfolds as a high-intensity psychological slasher built on a "whodunit" engine, *Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale* shifts the lens entirely. It functions as a "howdunit" - a reverse mystery that examines how catastrophe begins, tracing the emotional, cultural, and institutional forces that make tragedy inevitable long before anyone asks who is responsible.

Set eighteen months earlier, the novel trades immediacy for inevitability, drawing readers into the suffocating atmosphere of Excelsior Academy.

*A Grim Tale* is a genre-bending psychological thriller blending dark academia with Caribbean Gothic sensibilities. Written for mature young adult and crossover readers, the story grounds its horror in Dominican history, institutional culture, and the emotional reality of growing up inside systems built on silence and control.

*"The writing leans intensely cinematic, with emotions that feel oversized, raw, and volatile." - Literary Titan*

**A Different Kind of Terror**

Readers expecting the pace of Book 1 will find something heavier here - less spectacle, more psychological reckoning. Rather than asking who commits the violence, the prequel examines the slow pressure that makes violence feel inevitable.

*"This isn't a story about who did it - it's about how a system slowly makes tragedy unavoidable," says Torres De la Rocha.*

The project emerged directly from reader feedback after the first novel, expanding emotional and mythological layers that had only been hinted at before.

*"Readers didn't just react to Mortal Vengeance - they challenged me to look deeper. A Grim Tale became the space where I could explore the Grim Cojuelo's birth not as a monster, but as the consequence of repression and cruelty."*

**The Link to Mortal Vengeance: Before Everything Broke**

Designed to deepen the original novel without replacing it, *A Grim Tale* explores the systems that normalize cruelty and the quiet moments that later fracture lives. The story follows Julián - briefly referenced in the first book - whose perspective reveals how institutional pressure and moral compromise converge around a single student. Alongside him is Lucía, a fierce and loyal counterpart whose presence grounds the narrative emotionally.

Readers encounter earlier versions of familiar figures from the original novel, glimpsing them before identities harden and choices become irreversible - not as spoilers, but as echoes of who they once were.

*"Told in alternating points of view through beautiful, incisive prose." - Reader Views*

**The World Around Excelsior**

The prequel widens the lens beyond the students themselves, revealing the adult structures that shape their reality. Family expectations, authority figures, and institutional rituals form a quiet architecture of power - embodied by commanding parental forces, toxic relationships hinted at in the original novel, and a faculty whose guidance often blurs into control. Rather than presenting villains, the narrative exposes how systems - religious, academic, and social - create an environment where silence becomes survival.

**Darkness With Purpose**

*Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale* confronts difficult themes with intention, including grooming, institutional abuse, psychological trauma, coercive authority, suicide, domestic violence, machismo, and undiagnosed Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD). The novel does not pursue shock value; it examines how systems shape emotional collapse.

*"The story demanded darkness - but darkness without responsibility becomes spectacle," Torres De la Rocha explains.*

Religion plays a central role in the narrative. The story does not attack faith itself but questions how authority can be weaponized to justify cruelty or enforce silence. Reader discretion is advised.

**Folklore, Culture, and the Origin of the Grim Cojuelo**

Rooted deeply in Dominican culture, the novel reimagines the Grim Cojuelo - inspired by carnival folklore - not as a prankster, but as a symbolic embodiment of guilt, fear, and reckoning. The supernatural and psychological coexist in a way that keeps readers questioning where myth ends and reality begins.

*"The line between the supernatural and reality becomes blurred, making the horror far more terrifying than any jump scare." - Reader Views*

**Reading Order: Two Ways In**

*Mortal Vengeance* and *Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale* are designed to be experienced in either order:

- Beginning with *Mortal Vengeance* emphasizes mystery and revelation.
- Beginning with *A Grim Tale* reframes the original novel as consequence rather than surprise.

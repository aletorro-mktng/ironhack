# Mortal Vengeance Series Overview

Mortal Vengeance is a thriller and horror series by Alejandro Torres De la Rocha.

The series blends revenge, psychological suspense, Caribbean Gothic atmosphere, dark academia, Dominican folklore, institutional corruption, media spectacle, and morally complicated characters.

The series includes:

- Mortal Vengeance
- Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale
- Mortal Vengeance II: To Reel or Not Too Real?

The series is built around trauma, silence, guilt, survival, revenge, truth, and the cost of exposing what powerful people would rather bury.

## Core Series Identity

Mortal Vengeance is not a generic slasher series.

It combines:

- Psychological thriller tension
- Slasher horror structure
- Caribbean Gothic atmosphere
- Dominican folklore
- Dark academia
- Revenge tragedy
- Media satire
- Social and institutional critique

The horror does not only come from killers, blood, or bodies. It also comes from schools, families, reputations, silence, pressure, and the systems that protect themselves before they protect people.

## The Grim Cojuelo

A central mythic figure in the series is the Grim Cojuelo.

The Grim Cojuelo connects Dominican folklore, punishment, spectacle, guilt, and institutional rot. It is not just a monster or killer image. It represents the way buried wrongs return, the way guilt collects interest, and the way silence eventually demands payment.

## Marketing Positioning

Marketing content should position the series as:

- Caribbean Gothic horror
- Dark YA psychological thriller
- Slasher thriller
- Revenge thriller
- Dark academia horror
- Dominican folklore horror
- Media spectacle thriller

The content should emphasize the series’ specific cultural identity, sharp voice, morally complicated characters, and mix of horror, drama, satire, and emotional fallout.

## What to Avoid

Avoid positioning the series as:

- Generic horror
- Generic YA thriller
- Simple revenge fantasy
- Basic serial killer fiction
- Standard dark academia without cultural specificity
- A story where vengeance is clean or consequence-free

Mortal Vengeance should always feel specific, dangerous, emotional, and rooted in a world where every secret has teeth.

# Awards and Recognitions

This file contains verified awards, recognitions, honors, finalist placements, longlist/shortlist mentions, rankings, badges, media selections, festival selections, and other public proof points for the Mortal Vengeance series and related Tell Tales Ink projects.

Use this file as the source of truth for award and recognition claims.

Do not invent awards, recognitions, placements, rankings, badges, nominations, finalist status, winner status, or media selections.

Do not infer an award from a review, rating, quote, or general praise.

If an award or recognition is not listed here, it must be treated as unverified.

---

# Usage Rules

## Allowed

- Mention awards and recognitions exactly as listed.
- Include the award year, category, book/project, and result when provided.
- Use verified recognitions in press releases, media kits, captions, newsletters, blogs, and podcast scripts.
- State uncertainty only when the entry itself is marked as pending verification.

## Not Allowed

- Do not invent award names.
- Do not invent categories.
- Do not invent years.
- Do not upgrade a nomination, longlist, shortlist, finalist placement, or honorable mention into a win.
- Do not claim bestseller status unless listed here or in another verified source file.
- Do not present private praise, reader reviews, or editorial comments as awards.

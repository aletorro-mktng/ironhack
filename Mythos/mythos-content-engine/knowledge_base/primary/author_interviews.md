### Key Themes

## Interview INT-002: "Honesty and Exploitation"

### Source

Literary Titan

### Published URL

https://literarytitan.com/2026/03/08/honesty-and-exploitation/

### Main Book / Project

*Mortal Vengeance: A Grim Tale*

### Key Themes

- *A Grim Tale* was inspired partly by beta-reader feedback that the Grim Cojuelo lore, psychological drivers, and Profesora Lourdes' cruelty needed more development.
- The author drew from memories of twelve years at an all-boys Jesuit school: discipline language, moral framing, character formation rhetoric, and humiliation disguised as moral instruction.
- The book explores the Grim Cojuelo's birth as the consequence of repression and cruelty.
- The author describes the hardest part of psychological thriller writing as control, especially around suicide, grooming, institutional abuse, and psychological collapse.
- The book must be dark without becoming exploitative.
- Setting and atmosphere are treated as story infrastructure, not decoration.
- Excelsior Academy functions as a character and pressure cooker.
- The sequel, *Mortal Vengeance II: To Reel or Not Too Real?*, is described as a satire-inflected continuation about media, performance, spectacle, and truth.
- The author is also developing a children's series titled *IMALIVE*.

### Approved Author Quotes

"There is a thin line between honesty and exploitation."

"The most rewarding aspect is precision."

"You have to paint a picture. If the reader can't see it, they won't feel it."

"Description isn't decoration-it's infrastructure."

"Atmosphere isn't separate from the plot. It is plot."

"If it doesn't, it's just pretty writing. And pretty writing has no business in horror unless it's hiding something."

"This series has always interrogated power structures-this time, it asks what happens when violence, trauma, and justice become content."

"If *Mortal Vengeance* explores what happens when institutions silence young people, *IMALIVE* is about empowering children to believe their voices matter."

### Public-Safe Talking Points

- Use this interview for discussions of responsible darkness, psychological horror, institutional critique, and atmospheric craft.
- The interview is especially useful for *A Grim Tale* press, podcast scripts, blog posts, and author Q&A content.
- Future timing language should remain tentative: the author says Book 2 may arrive "perhaps fall of 2026" if all goes according to plan.

## Interview INT-003: "Alejandro Torres, author of Mortal Vengeance"

### Source

BookView Review

### Published URL

https://bookviewreview.com/2026/03/07/interview-with-author-alejandro-torres-de-la-rocha/

### Main Book / Project

*Mortal Vengeance*

### Key Themes

- Writing is therapeutic because it helps the author organize thoughts, understand feelings, and release emotions.
- The author discusses ego, feedback, and how listening to readers led to the B-Roll chapter and later to *A Grim Tale*.
- The author balances originality and reader expectations by focusing on the best story possible rather than trying to please everyone.
- The author acknowledges *Scream*, *I Know What You Did Last Summer*, and *Teaching Mrs. Tingle* as influences while emphasizing unique elements: Dominican setting, character depth, villain motive, B-Roll, and bringing visual mediums into prose.
- Some characters are inspired by real people, but fictionalization, discretion, and respect matter.
- Character and plot should work together rather than being treated as an either/or.
- The author values revision, beta-reader patterns, clarity without over-explaining, and restraint with metaphor.
- The author is candid about the challenges of self-publishing, visibility, reviews, and industry gatekeeping.

### Approved Author Quotes

"It doesn't fix them, but it releases them-and that matters."

"Everything in excess is bad, and ego is no exception."

"It's not one or the other. It's both."

"Distractions don't just steal time-they fragment attention."

"Give me something I can work with."

"Writing the book is only the first battle."

"Mortal Vengeance was his personal auto-correct. And a warning shot."

Alejandro Torres De la Rocha is Dominican-born and raised, based in Barcelona, and works across writing, training, and creative strategy. He holds a Bachelor's in Advertising Communications from UNIBE and a Master of Science in Public Relations and Corporate Communications from NYU.

## Interview INT-004: Book Nerdection Author Interview

### Source

Book Nerdection

### Published URL

https://booknerdection.com/behind-the-pages-author-interview-with-alejandro-torres-de-la-rocha/

### Main Book / Project

*Mortal Vengeance*

### Key Themes

- *Mortal Vengeance* began from rage, boredom, and a desire for petty revenge after a D- in Spanish and a punishment that removed TV, Nintendo 64, and dial-up internet.
- The original version was a screenplay written during freshman year in the late 1990s.
- The author was influenced by Kevin Williamson-era slasher storytelling and the combination of suspense, sharp dialogue, and self-awareness.
- The author sees thrillers as a way to strip away social masks and reveal who people become under pressure.
- Alex was the most interesting character to write because he began as a teenage self-projection and became a tool for self-reflection.
- One character who was originally supposed to die survived after revision and reader feedback, reshaping the sequel.
- The author grounded later revisions in reality and modernized the threat through technology, social media, smart devices, and erosion of trust.
- The author wanted the killer's motivation to be understandable but not justified.
- Reader feedback is meaningful because writing is solitary and a strong reader reaction confirms that the emotional connection is working.
- The author's process starts with obsession, then structure, character work, drafting, distance, and revision.
- The sequel *Mortal Vengeance II: To Reel or Not Too Real?* explores media, technology, entertainment, manipulated reality, and performance.
- The author plans a children's series called *IMALIVE* about imagination, perspective, and protecting creative agency.

### Approved Author Quotes

"The honest truth? The book was born from pure rage, boredom, and a desire for petty revenge."

"It's not really about the mystery of who did it. It's about watching people become themselves under pressure."

"Fear changes behavior."

"Understanding is not the same as justification."

"My process starts with obsession."

"More than anything, I hope they feel unsettled in the best possible way."

"If the story stays with them for a while after they close the book, then it has done its job."

# Cross-Interview Positioning

## Author Origin Story

Alejandro began writing *Mortal Vengeance* as a teenager after being punished for a bad Spanish grade. With distractions removed, anger and boredom became creative fuel. Across interviews, he frames the book's origin as petty revenge transformed into craft, emotional release, and a disciplined psychological thriller.

## Core Creative Philosophy

- Writing is emotional release, but revision turns emotion into craft.
- Horror works when atmosphere, character, and plot all serve each other.
- Systems, institutions, and silence are central to the Mortal Vengeance universe.
- Monsters are not random; they are shaped by environments that fail people.
- Reader feedback can deepen the work without forcing the author to abandon the story's identity.

## Recurring Themes

- Institutional silence
- Jesuit school discipline and moral rhetoric
- Dominican culture and class hierarchy
- Psychological horror
- Slasher influence
- Moral ambiguity
- The cost of revenge
- Media spectacle
- Technology and manipulation
- Character psychology under pressure
- Imagination and agency

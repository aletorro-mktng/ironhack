# YouTube Best Practices

## YouTube Titles

A title has one job: earn the click without misleading the viewer.

### Best Practices

* **Keep titles under 60 characters when possible.**
* **Lead with the most important keyword or topic.**
* **Focus on curiosity, benefit, or outcome.**
* **Be specific rather than generic.**
* **Avoid clickbait that the video cannot deliver.**
* **Use numbers, questions, comparisons, or strong outcomes when relevant.**
* **Front-load important words since mobile devices often truncate titles.**

### Effective Examples

* How I Wrote a 700-Page Thriller
* Mortal Vengeance Won YA Horror. Here's What Happened.
* The Biggest Mistake Indie Authors Make
* ChatGPT vs Claude for Content Marketing
* I Tried Publishing a Novel in 2026

### Avoid

* WATCH THIS NOW!!!
* You Won't Believe This...
* My Thoughts
* Random Update Video

The viewer should immediately understand what the video is about and why they should care.

## YouTube Descriptions

The description helps YouTube understand the video, improves discoverability, and provides context for viewers.

### Structure

#### Opening Summary (First 2-3 Sentences)

The first 150-200 characters are the most important because they appear in search results and above the "Show More" fold.

Answer:

* What is this video about?
* Why should someone watch?

Example:

"In this video, I break down how Mortal Vengeance won the 2026 IndieReader Discovery Award for YA Horror, what the judging process was like, and what indie authors can learn from the experience."

#### Main Body

Expand on:

* Key topics covered.
* Important takeaways.
* Resources mentioned.
* Related context.

Write naturally while including relevant keywords.

#### Links & Resources

Include:

* Website
* Newsletter
* Book links
* Social media
* Related videos
* Affiliate links (when applicable)

#### Timestamps

For longer videos, add chapter markers.

Example:

00:00 Introduction
02:15 The Award Announcement
05:42 Why This Book Was Different
10:11 Lessons for Indie Authors
15:37 Final Thoughts

## SEO Best Practices

### Primary Keyword Placement

Include your primary keyword in:

* Title
* First sentence of description
* Video filename (before upload if possible)
* Spoken dialogue when natural
* Chapters and timestamps when relevant

### Secondary Keywords

Naturally incorporate related phrases and variations throughout the description.

Do not keyword stuff.

### Search Intent

Match what viewers are searching for:

* How-to
* Review
* Comparison
* Tutorial
* Commentary
* News
* Analysis
* Storytelling

The title and description should immediately signal which category the video belongs to.

## Engagement Optimization

### Encourage Viewer Actions

Ask viewers to:

* Subscribe
* Comment
* Like
* Watch another video
* Visit a website
* Join a newsletter

Use only one primary CTA whenever possible.

### Increase Watch Time

Set clear expectations early.

Tell viewers:

* What they will learn.
* What they will see.
* Why it matters.

A confused viewer is a viewer preparing to leave.

---

## AI & Discovery Optimization

Modern YouTube discovery increasingly relies on understanding content rather than matching exact keywords.

To improve discoverability:

* Use clear language.
* Answer questions directly.
* Write descriptive titles.
* Create detailed descriptions.
* Use accurate chapters.
* Include relevant entities, people, products, books, places, and topics.

Optimize for understanding, not keyword density.

The goal is simple:

**A human should instantly understand the video's value, and YouTube should instantly understand the video's topic.**

## **Subject Lines & Open Rates**

Strong subject lines improve open rates by setting clear expectations and creating curiosity without becoming clickbait.

### **Best Practices**

* **Keep subject lines under 50 characters.** Make them concise, specific, and easy to scan.  
* **Use preview text strategically.** The preheader should complement the subject line and tease the value inside the email.  
* **Avoid spam triggers.** Do not use all caps, excessive punctuation, misleading claims, or overly image-heavy email layouts.

## **Content & Value**

Every email should have a clear purpose and deliver value quickly.

### **Best Practices**

* **Use one clear call-to-action.** Each email should focus on one primary goal, such as reading an article, buying a product, registering for an event, or replying to a question.  
* **Personalize when possible.** Use the reader’s first name and segment subscriber lists based on interests, behavior, or customer stage.  
* **Provide original value.** Do not simply repeat news or website content. Add unique insights, case studies, commentary, recommendations, or actionable advice subscribers cannot easily find elsewhere.
